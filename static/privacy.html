When you submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect your name, email address, WhatsApp phone number and requested service.

If WhatsApp messaging features are enabled, we may also process your phone number, messages and related conversation information.

Information may be stored in our application database and processed through service providers used to operate LeadFlow AI, such as hosting, database, automation, email and spreadsheet services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the service, respond to enquiries or comply with legal obligations.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. You can request access, correction or deletion of your information using our Data Deletion page.

7. Your Choices

You may choose not to submit information through our website. You can also request that we correct or delete information you have provided.

We may update this policy when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
