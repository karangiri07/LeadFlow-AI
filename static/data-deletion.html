You have the right to request deletion of personal information you have shared with LeadFlow AI.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Send an email to our support address.
  2. Use the subject line: LeadFlow AI - Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your name and the email address or phone number associated with your enquiry.
  4. Tell us that you want your personal information deleted.

Request Data Deletion

What Happens Next?

We will verify your request where reasonably necessary and identify the information associated with your account or enquiry.

We will delete or anonymize eligible information from our active systems within 30 days of verifying your request, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Third-Party Services

If information has been processed by service providers such as email, automation or spreadsheet platforms, we will take reasonable steps to address the deletion request there where applicable and within our control.
